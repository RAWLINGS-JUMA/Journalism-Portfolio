Personal & Academic Journey

From curiosity to committed practice.

My journey into journalism began with an interest in how broadcast reports reach an audience and why clear, verified reporting feels immediate, trustworthy and human.

That interest has grown into practical work through an internship at MMU Radio 99.9 FM Kajiado, where I am learning from a working broadcast environment.

I am continuing to develop the craft behind the work: research, structure, interviewing, editing and the responsibility that comes with speaking to the public.

Journalism Philosophy

Accuracy first. People always.

I believe good journalism starts with respect for the truth and for the people involved in a report. Facts must be checked, context must be clear and voices must be represented fairly.

As a broadcast journalist, I want my work to be calm, clear and evidence-led — whether I am presenting a bulletin, conducting an interview or shaping a short news package.

Ethical judgement is not an extra step; it belongs at the centre of every editorial decision.
